## Probably coincidental, or don't copy

- Accessibility or fairness framing in all three. n = 3.
- Large stacks (voice, ClamAV, JWS, OTel). KeepMe's heavier stack placed below ShadeSpan's leaner one.
- Video length: 2:55, 2:55, 2:05. Not a differentiator.
- **A visible third-party brand (Aloud) and a fixture-only demo (KeepMe) placed anyway.** Neither is a licence to
  do the same: the rules restrict trademarks and require footage of the project functioning.
