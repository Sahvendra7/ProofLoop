# ProofLoop — Live Demo Script

**Total Demo Time**: ~2 minutes 30 seconds

### 0:00 - Introduction (Landing Page)
*"Skincare shopping today is broken. We buy products based on marketing, use them for a month, and have no idea if they actually changed anything. ProofLoop fixes this by turning skincare into a measurable, continuous loop."*
**Action**: Scroll through the landing page briefly, highlighting the "How ProofLoop Works" trust layer. 

### 0:20 - Step 1: Scan (Scan Page)
*"It starts with a scan. Instead of a generic quiz, we use YouCam's Skin Analysis API to get an objective measurement of your skin."*
**Action**: Click "Upload", select a selfie, and wait for the analysis.
*(Note the loading state clearly showing that work is being done).*

### 0:40 - Step 2: Understand & Choose (Profile Page)
*"Here is our profile. The raw data doesn't overwhelm the user. We translate the YouCam AI signals into a clear summary of what this means, and ask the user to choose what they actually want to improve."*
**Action**: Highlight the "What this means" summary. Select "Hydration" as the goal and click "Find My Matches".

### 1:05 - Step 3: Explainable Shop (Recommendations Page)
*"This is where the magic happens. We deterministically match the user's specific YouCam metrics to our product catalogue. Notice the 'Why this matches you' section — we explain exactly how this product fits their baseline signals and their specific goal. No black-box AI hallucinations."*
**Action**: Scroll through the product list. Click "View Product & Goal" on the top match.

### 1:30 - Step 4: Visualize Goal (Product Page)
*"Before they buy, we show them a conceptual visualization of their goal. Not a guarantee, but a target. But the most important button is at the bottom: 'Save Baseline & Buy'."*
**Action**: Click the "Simulate Goal" button. Then click "Save Baseline & Buy".

### 1:55 - Step 5: The Loop (Journey Page - Day 0)
*"When they buy, we save their scan as Day 0. In a real scenario, they use the product for 3 weeks and come back."*
**Action**: Click "Fast Forward to Day 21".

### 2:10 - Step 6: Verify (Journey Page - Day 21)
*"This is the closed loop. By comparing their Day 21 YouCam scan to their Day 0 baseline, we provide objective, mathematical proof of how their skin has changed. They don't have to guess anymore. They have proof."*
**Action**: Highlight the deltas showing exact point improvements.

### 2:30 - Conclusion
*"That's ProofLoop: Measure, Shop, Track, Verify."*
